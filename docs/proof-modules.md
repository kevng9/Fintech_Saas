# Proof modules beyond the anchors

A1–A8 prove fintech use cases. These additional proof modules close two
objections that often appear after a strong use-case demo:

| ID | Name | Audience | Duration | Objection removed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P9 | Why Aura, not a generic LLM | Business + technical buyers | ~90 seconds | “Isn’t this just ChatGPT on our data?” |
| P10 | AI Platform Economics | CFO, FP&A, platform economics | 2–3 minutes | “What happens to AI OPEX as usage scales?” |

In-app talk track and show path: [`/value`](../app/src/app/value/page.tsx) (route `/value`).
Full module cards also live in
[`fintech_aura_analyst_modular_demo_playbook.md`](../fintech_aura_analyst_modular_demo_playbook.md).

## When to insert P9

After the first live or reviewed Analyst answer in any anchor. Do not open the
meeting with it — land a business outcome first, then differentiate.

**One-line close:** *Governed context. Live SQL. Human decision.*

## When to insert P10

When a CFO, FP&A, or platform-economics buyer is in the room, or when the
customer volunteers AI/warehouse spend concern. Pair with the Platform page.
Never invent competitive invoices.

**One-line close:** *Architecture decides whether AI spend compounds.*

## Suggested 10-minute business recipe with differentiation

1. Framing (1)
2. Anchor show — usually Payment Performance Management or Digital Funnel Performance (5)
3. **P9 Why Aura** (1.5)
4. Dialogue + stage gate (2.5)

## Suggested CFO / FP&A recipe

1. Framing: AI is becoming operating spend (1)
2. **P10 cost architecture** (3)
3. Short Payment Performance Management or Liquidity show as the “current data” proof (5)
4. Gate: bring AI + warehouse baseline to a value workshop (1)
